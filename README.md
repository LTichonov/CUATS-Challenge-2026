# CUATS-Challenge-2026

You may find all of the relevant material for my submission to the Cambridge University Algorithmic Trading Society's (CUATS's) 2026 Coding Challenge, which earnt Second Place. 

The algorithm is based on [this paper](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/17/11/501?). This low frequency strategy was implemented successfully on QuantConnect, and its universe changed from the SPY index to the top 40 SNP 500 Constituents (as of Jan 2026). This increases the trading frequency of the strategy making it useable in the contest's 6-week live trading period. The strategy's performance is not great, but given the contest judged on clean-ness of code, originality, quality of idea and quality of presentation (file in repo), it performed well in the contest.
